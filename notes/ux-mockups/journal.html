Bed journal: every cell's history, summarized

Tap the sheet's handle (or drag it up) to open the journal for where you stand. At the bed it covers all 50 cells. The Cells tab is the default: one card per cell, most recent activity first, with care counts and flags such as "dry 3 days". Rows and Columns fold the cells' events back into the commands that made them ("Watered A1–A8") with a strip showing which cells each reached. Every card and section is a way in: tap it to zoom the map there.

Landscape

The side sheet widens into the journal; the whole bed stays visible on the left.
